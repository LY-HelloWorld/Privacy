This policy governs snippets, templates, categories, and other text you create in Quick Content Keyboard. It supplements our Terms of Use and Privacy Policy.

Keep secrets elsewhere. Do not store passwords, payment-card details, recovery keys, one-time-password seeds, government identifiers, or confidential credentials in the App.

1. Your responsibility

You control and retain rights in your content. You are responsible for accuracy, legality, permissions, and reviewing a snippet before inserting or sending it. Template substitutions may produce unintended text if values are incomplete or incorrect.

2. Where content appears

Content may appear in the main App, its keyboard extension, a JSON backup you export, and the active text field after you select a snippet. The App does not provide a public feed, team workspace, or developer-operated content server.

3. Prohibited content and use

You must not use the App to create, store, insert, or distribute content that violates law; infringes intellectual property, privacy, publicity, or contractual rights; facilitates fraud, phishing, malware, credential theft, harassment, threats, hate, exploitation, or unlawful discrimination; impersonates another person; or misrepresents authorization or affiliation.

4. Third-party destinations

When you insert text into another app or export a backup, that destination’s rules and privacy practices apply. The availability and behavior of custom keyboards are controlled partly by iOS and the destination app.

5. No routine monitoring

Because user content is designed to remain on your device and we do not operate content storage servers, we do not routinely receive or review it. We may respond to information voluntarily sent to support or to valid legal requests.

Do not store or distribute material you lack permission to use. The App, branding, and documentation remain protected by applicable intellectual-property laws.
