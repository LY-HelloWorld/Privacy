Quick Content Keyboard

Save the text you use every day. Insert it from your iPhone keyboard.

Keep common replies, signatures, and reusable templates in one place, then select a snippet while writing in an app that supports custom keyboards.

Setup guide & support

Less repeated typing

  • Reusable snippets: save frequently used text and find it by category.
  • Templates: prepare fixed details and selectable options in the main app. Preview filled text before inserting it from the keyboard.
  • Dates and times: use supported date and time fields in templates and adjust dates when needed.
  • Optional identity checks: require system authentication before viewing or using selected snippets.
  • Backup: export and import your content and template details as a JSON file.

Designed to work locally

Your snippets stay on your device. There is no account to create, and the keyboard does not request Full Access. The app includes no advertising or third-party analytics SDK. Learn more in our Privacy Policy.

Start free, unlock more with Pro

The free version supports up to 3 snippets and 1 category. An optional one-time Pro purchase unlocks unlimited snippets and categories, template variables, identity checks, and JSON import and export. The app displays the price provided by the App Store for your region.

Works with your language

The app and keyboard support English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Japanese, Korean, French, German, Spanish, and Brazilian Portuguese.

Getting started

Create a snippet in the main app, enable Quick Content Keyboard in iOS Settings, then switch keyboards using the globe key. See the support page for steps and common questions.
