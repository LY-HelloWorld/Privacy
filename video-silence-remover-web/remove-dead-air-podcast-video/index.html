Editing a long podcast or interview can mean listening for every awkward gap. Video Silence Remover helps create a shorter cut from the silent sections, then gives you a preview before export.

Common questions

Can it remove all the dead air from a podcast?

It is intended to remove silent sections from a video. Results depend on the recording, background noise, and the app's current detection behavior, so preview the output before publishing.

Will it remove meaningful pauses?

Long pauses can carry meaning in an interview. Treat automatic silence removal as a first pass and review the shorter cut before saving.

Can I use the result for Shorts or Reels?

You can save or share the processed video from iPhone, then continue in the app you use for publishing.
