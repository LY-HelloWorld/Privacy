Questions people have before removing pauses

Will it remove every pause?

The goal is to remove silent sections, not to erase every natural break in speech. Preview the result so you can check that the pacing still sounds right.

Is this for regular video editing?

It is focused on one task: shortening videos by removing silence and dead air. It is useful when a full timeline editor feels like too much work.

Where does the video get processed?

The app is designed for local, on-device processing. See the app's privacy policy for the current data handling details.
