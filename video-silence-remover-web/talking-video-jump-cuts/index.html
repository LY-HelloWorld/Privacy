What to check before you export

Will the jump cuts sound natural?

That depends on the recording and how much silence it contains. Listen to the preview before exporting; meaningful pauses may be better left in.

Does this replace a full video editor?

No. It is a focused tool for removing silence and creating a tighter first cut. You can continue editing elsewhere after saving or sharing.
