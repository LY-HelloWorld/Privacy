User Content and Acceptable Use Policy

Effective date: October 1, 2026 · App: Pet Photo Assistant · Bundle ID: com.luoyi.PetPhotoAssistant

This User Content and Acceptable Use Policy ("Content Policy") explains your responsibilities for photos, custom recordings, and other material used with Pet Photo Assistant.

No public feed or hosted upload. The current App is a local photography utility. It does not provide a public profile, social feed, developer-hosted content library, messaging feature, or publishing service. Your photos and custom recording are not uploaded to us by the App.

1. Your Content and Ownership

"User Content" includes photos you capture, photos you select and save, a custom attention sound you record, and any people, animals, property, artwork, trademarks, or private information appearing in that material.

You retain the rights you already have in your User Content. The App's local processing does not transfer ownership to us. You are responsible for your User Content and for how you create, keep, or use it.

2. Consent and Third-Party Rights

You must have a lawful basis and all permissions needed to photograph or record people, animals, property, and private spaces. Respect copyrights, trademarks, privacy rights, publicity rights, confidentiality obligations, venue rules, and requests not to be photographed or recorded.

Do not record another person's voice or capture a person in a private or sensitive context without consent where consent is required. Pet ownership does not necessarily grant permission to photograph another person's home, belongings, documents, or personal information.

3. Prohibited Content and Uses

You may not use the App to create, organize, facilitate, or support:

  • content or conduct that violates applicable law;
  • material that infringes intellectual-property, privacy, publicity, confidentiality, or other rights;
  • non-consensual intimate, sexual, exploitative, voyeuristic, or otherwise invasive content;
  • child sexual abuse or exploitation material, grooming, or any sexualized depiction of a minor;
  • harassment, stalking, doxxing, threats, hate, intimidation, or abuse;
  • animal cruelty, neglect, exploitation, staged distress, dangerous restraint, or deliberate provocation;
  • fraud, impersonation, evidence tampering, or misleading use of images intended to harm another person;
  • instructions or coordination for serious harm, unlawful surveillance, or other dangerous conduct.

4. Safe and Humane Pet Photography

Use attention sounds sparingly and at a safe volume. Stop if an animal shows fear, stress, pain, aggression, avoidance, or discomfort. Do not use the App to provoke a reaction, force an unsafe pose, interfere with feeding or medical care, place an animal near hazards, or distract a working or service animal.

Prioritize the safety of animals and people over obtaining a photograph. Keep your device secure, maintain awareness of your surroundings, and follow advice from owners, handlers, veterinarians, and relevant authorities.

5. Local Processing and Visibility

Live camera frames and captured photos are processed on your device to recognize pets and assess possible photo quality. A custom attention sound is stored locally in the App. Anyone with access to your unlocked device may be able to view content shown by the App or Photos, subject to your device settings.

Because the current App does not host or receive your User Content, we do not routinely view, screen, moderate, restore, or remove your private on-device material.

6. Saving, Copies, and Deletion

Unsaved captured photos are temporary to the current App session. When you select and save a photo, a copy is added to the system Photos library and must be managed there. Deleting the App does not delete photos already saved to Photos. If iCloud Photos is enabled, Apple may synchronize saved photos according to your settings.

You can replace or delete the custom recording in the App. You are responsible for other copies you create, including screenshots, backups, exports, or copies made by system services.

7. Enforcement and Legal Requests

The App has no public hosting or user account to suspend. We may update or discontinue the App, revise this Content Policy, respond to valid legal requirements, or take other lawful action if we become aware that the App is being misused. Because we do not operate a content-upload service for the App, we may be unable to locate or remove material stored only on a user's device.

8. Reporting Safety or Rights Concerns

If you believe the App itself infringes your rights or creates a safety concern, contact us with enough information to understand the issue. For immediate danger, suspected abuse, or unlawful material on a device, contact the appropriate local authority or qualified organization.

9. Related Policies

This Content Policy forms part of the App's Terms of Use. The Privacy Policy explains how camera frames, photos, recordings, permissions, and Apple services are handled.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Content Policy when App functionality, safety considerations, or applicable requirements change. We will post the revised version on this page with a new effective date.
