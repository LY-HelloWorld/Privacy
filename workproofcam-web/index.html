Local-first job content

Your job records are not a cloud collaboration feed.

Jobs, proof photos, notes, and generated reports are designed to stay on your device. WorkProofCam does not require an account and does not provide cloud sync in the current version.

Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics may process technical usage and diagnostic information to measure app flows and diagnose crashes. They are not used to upload job records, client or site names, proof photos, report notes, precise job coordinates, addresses, or PDF report content.

Questions before you download

Is WorkProofCam only for contractors?

No. It is useful for cleaning, inspections, repairs, installations, maintenance, and other field visits where photos and notes need to become a clear report.

Does the app upload my job photos?

WorkProofCam is designed to keep job content local. Your report or photos leave the app when you choose a destination through an iOS sharing flow. Technical analytics and crash diagnostics are described separately in the privacy policy.

Do I need a subscription?

No. The app is free to download, and the optional Pro upgrade is offered as a one-time in-app purchase.

Does a timestamp or GPS value guarantee legal proof?

No. Device time, location, and reverse-geocoded addresses can be unavailable or inaccurate. Review every report and confirm it meets the requirements of your client, employer, insurer, contract, or jurisdiction.
