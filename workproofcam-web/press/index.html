Product facts

A focused photo-to-report workflow

Use these facts when describing WorkProofCam. They intentionally avoid legal-certification and tamper-proof claims.

Capture

Keep context with the photo

Capture job photos with timestamps, optional GPS coordinates and address context, then add a note while the details are still fresh.

Organize

Show the work sequence

Group records as Before, After, or General proof photos inside the job instead of mixing them into the personal camera roll.

Deliver

Send one clear report

Review the job record and export a PDF. Individual original-size watermarked JPEG proof photos can also be shared or saved to Photos.

Positioning

For field evidence, not field operations.

WorkProofCam is designed for contractors, technicians, inspectors, cleaners, repair and maintenance workers, and small field-service teams that need a repeatable record without adopting a full construction-management platform.

Job content is local-first, no account is required, and the current app does not provide cloud collaboration. Technical analytics and crash diagnostics are described separately in the privacy policy.

Evidence limitation: timestamps, GPS values, addresses, watermarks, and generated reports organize the user's record. They do not cryptographically certify a photo or guarantee acceptance by a client, insurer, employer, contract, court, or jurisdiction.

Maker comment

Hi Product Hunt — I built WorkProofCam after researching recurring public discussions from contractors and field workers: existing-damage disputes, hundreds of job photos that become impossible to find later, notes separated from the right image, and reports that still need to be assembled after leaving the site. WorkProofCam keeps the workflow focused: capture the job, add time and optional location context, organize Before, After, and General proof, then export one clear PDF. Job content is local-first, no account is required, and Pro is a one-time purchase rather than a subscription. I would value feedback from people who document repairs, inspections, cleaning, installations, or other field work.
