The job content is designed to stay with the app on your device

That includes the material you create for a specific job and report.

Job records

Titles, clients, sites, and notes

The App stores the job details you enter using Apple on-device storage.

Proof content

Photos, labels, and photo notes

Captured JPEG files are kept in the App's private Application Support directory for display and report generation.

Report files

PDF output you control

A temporary PDF may be created for export. You choose the Mail, Files, AirDrop, Messages, cloud-storage, or other destination.

Local-first does not mean zero diagnostics

Firebase handles technical usage and crash data—not your job report content.

WorkProofCam uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to measure basic app usage, understand purchase-flow issues, diagnose crashes, and improve stability. These services may process device-level identifiers, product interaction events, purchase-status events, crash reports, stack traces, operating-system information, and app-version information.

Firebase is not used to upload job records, client or site names, proof photos, PDF reports, photo notes, precise job GPS coordinates, addresses, or other report content. WorkProofCam does not set a Firebase user ID from a name, email address, or app account.
