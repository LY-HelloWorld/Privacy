What a useful report can answer

The exact content depends on what you enter, capture, permit, and review.

Which job?

Recognizable job context

A title, client, site, and job notes can help the recipient identify the visit.

What changed?

Before-and-after observations

Proof labels and photo notes can explain the starting condition and completed result.

When and where?

Optional supporting context

Timestamps are included with photo records; GPS coordinates and a best-effort address depend on permission and availability.

Review before sharing
