Give each proof photo a role

The labels make the sequence readable without pretending the app can verify what happened.

Before

Record the starting condition

Capture the area, item, damage, installation point, or condition you need to reference before work begins.

After

Record the completed result

Show the finished work from a useful angle and add a note that explains what the client should notice.

General proof

Keep supporting details

Add materials, serial numbers, access conditions, intermediate steps, or other context that does not fit a direct pair.

Context is not certification

Review every timestamp, location, address, and note.

When permission is granted, WorkProofCam can attach GPS coordinates and a best-effort reverse-geocoded address. Device settings, signal quality, Apple services, and site conditions can affect those values.

A clearer sequence for the person receiving the report

The report should answer three basic questions without a separate explanation.

  1. 01

    Which job?

    Use a specific title plus client and site details that the recipient recognizes.

  2. 02

    What was visible?

    Use the before photo and note to show the starting point.

  3. 03

    What changed?

    Use the after photo and note to make the completed result understandable.

  4. 04

    What supports it?

    Review timestamps, optional location context, and general proof before export.
