Private by architecture

Media analysis and rendering use Apple frameworks on the device. The app has no developer-operated photo server, account system, advertising SDK, or analytics SDK. A first verified protected export is included; further exports require a one-time Lifetime Access purchase through Apple.

Know the boundary

Automatic detection can miss sensitive details. Always review the still, scrub through motion, confirm the audio choice, and consider contextual clues before sharing. No privacy tool can guarantee anonymity.
