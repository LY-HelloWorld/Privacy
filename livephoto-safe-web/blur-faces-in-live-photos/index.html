Motion redaction guide

Blur the face through the Live Photo—not only on the cover

A person can move outside a static blur as soon as the Live Photo starts playing.

The motion gap

Editors built for still images can make the key photo look protected while leaving the paired motion untouched. For Live Photos, the mask must be evaluated over time.

How LivePhoto Safe approaches it

On-device face detection suggests regions, and motion tracking carries selected masks through sampled motion. You can add, remove, resize, and review regions before rendering a new copy.

Manual review still matters

Tracking can drift when a face turns, leaves the frame, is blocked, or moves quickly. Scrub the timeline, watch entrances and exits, and use a stronger mask or avoid sharing when the result is uncertain.

Beyond faces

Names, addresses, screens, barcodes, license details, and reflections may identify someone even when a face is hidden. Review the complete frame and context.
