Will AI make my grandparents look different?

Any enhancement can estimate missing pixels. ClearPix gives you a before-and-after comparison so you can reject a result that feels unnatural.

Can it repair deep scratches or missing areas?

It can improve clarity and reduce visible noise, but it cannot guarantee recovery of detail that is completely missing.

Can I restore several family photos?

ClearPix supports batch enhancement for up to five photos in the current workflow.

Are old photos sent to a server?

No. Photo processing is designed to happen locally on your device.
