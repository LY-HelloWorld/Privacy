A few things you might wonder.

Why does an evening photo look grainy?

Low light can leave visible speckles, especially in shadows and smooth backgrounds. Noise cleanup helps make the subject easier to enjoy.

Which option suits a dark indoor picture?

Use Low-Light when the picture needs more visibility. Choose Light for grain in an otherwise well-lit picture, or Strong for grain with softness.

Can I compare the atmosphere of both versions?

Yes. Move the comparison slider and choose the balance of brightness, texture, and mood you prefer.

Where can I use the saved picture?

Save it to your library, ready for a message, a shared album, or your next post.
