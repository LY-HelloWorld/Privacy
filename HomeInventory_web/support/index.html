What usually goes wrong

  • You packed the item, but cannot remember which box holds it.
  • Several labels say the same room and no longer narrow the search.
  • A box changed, but the people helping you still have an older list.

How Moving Boxes Organizer helps

Keep item names, photos, notes, and the matching box together, then search or scan the label when you need the record.

Common questions

Get the box record working for you again.

The problem

I am packing my first box. Where do I start?

What to do

Create a box, give it a name and room, then add item names as you pack. Add a photo or note only when it will help you recognize something later.

The problem

I know the item is packed, but not where.

What to do

Search the item name and open the matching box record. Check the room and notes before opening the physical box.

The problem

The outside label is too vague.

What to do

Print a label from the box record and attach it to the matching box. Scanning it opens that local record, so the outside label does not need to list every item.

The problem

My family needs the same up-to-date list.

What to do

Choose the inventory you want to share and invite the people helping you. Only put non-sensitive boxes in a shared inventory; keep private records in My Inventory.

The problem

My Pro purchase is not showing.

What to do

Open the Pro purchase screen and choose Restore Purchases while signed in to the Apple Account used for the original purchase.

The problem

I need to know where my inventory is kept.

How it works

Your inventory stays on your device by default. If you explicitly use Shared Inventory, the invited people can collaborate on the non-sensitive records you place there.
