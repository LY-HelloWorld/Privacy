Tax-presence reference

US and UK tax-presence workspaces

StayCount provides separate workspaces for US Substantial Presence and UK visitor and Statutory Residence Test (SRT) inputs and results. They use recorded travel stays and the rule facts entered in the relevant workspace.

US Substantial Presence

The US workspace shows the current-year count, weighted total across three years, the 31-day minimum, and fact inputs used in the review.

UK visitor and SRT

The UK workspaces record visitor and tax-year facts, including ties, work days, transit dates, and exceptional-circumstance dates, then present result explanation rows.

Planning-tool boundary

These workspaces do not determine a person's tax status and do not provide tax, legal, immigration, or financial advice. Results depend on the information entered and do not guarantee an outcome. Consult official guidance and a qualified adviser for decisions that matter to you.
