Travel-ledger reference

Private travel-day ledger

StayCount records travel stays through manual entry. A stay can include dates, a country entry, notes, and supporting files selected through the system file picker.

On-device records

Travel dates, country entries, rule facts, notes, attachment records, and the App Lock setting are stored in the app's local storage on the device.

Optional App Lock

When App Lock is enabled, the app requests device-owner authentication through iOS. The app receives an authentication result rather than biometric templates or biometric images.

Attachments and backup

Attachments are added when selected through the system file picker and are stored with their related stay. A user can initiate a password-encrypted backup and choose its destination through the system file exporter.

Planning-tool boundary

Travel records and rule results are planning support based on information entered in the app. StayCount does not provide immigration, legal, tax, or financial advice, and does not guarantee an outcome.
