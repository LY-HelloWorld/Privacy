Common questions

Why is the next destination locked?

Find three different keepsakes in the previous destination. The game unlocks the next place after you claim that progress.

Why did I not receive a keepsake?

Photos are part of the normal travel result, while keepsakes use the game’s increasing chance system. A trip without a keepsake increases the next opportunity. Longer trips and suitable luggage can improve the chance. Your journal and photo are still saved.

Can I leave the game while Little Chestnut is traveling?

Yes. The result is fixed when Little Chestnut leaves and the return time is saved locally. You can close the game and come back later. Local return reminders are optional.

How do I restore the full-game purchase?

Open the map or Settings, open the full-game invitation, and tap Restore Purchases while using the Apple Account that made the purchase. Apple handles eligible refunds and purchase history.

Will restoring a purchase restore my travel progress?

No. Purchase access and local travel progress are separate. Use the game’s JSON backup export and restore controls for travel progress.

What happens if a purchase is waiting for approval?

The game keeps the purchase pending and prevents a second purchase prompt. When Apple confirms it, access updates automatically. If the result is unclear, use Restore Purchases before trying again.

How do I turn off music or haptics?

Open the cottage Settings and use Sound & Haptics. Music, ambience, effects, and haptics have separate switches. Audio stops when the game leaves the foreground.

Before contacting us

Include the game version, device model, iOS version, and a short description of what happened. Please do not send your backup file, Apple Account details, payment information, or private travel notes unless support specifically asks for a safe, relevant detail.

For Apple billing or refund decisions, use Apple’s purchase support. We cannot see your payment card or change an Apple refund from inside the game.
