Little Chestnut's Journey Support

Need help with a trip, your save, or the Full Game purchase? Email luoyi9932@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, and a short description of the issue. Do not send payment card details.

Restore your purchase

Open Settings in the game, choose Full Game, then tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account that made the original purchase and connect to the internet. Restoring the purchase unlocks the paid destinations; it does not restore travel progress.

Back up or restore your travels

In game Settings, export a JSON save file to a location you choose. To move progress to another device, import that file there and confirm the replacement. Keep your backup in a safe place.

Play offline

City Park can be explored for free. A one-time Full Game purchase unlocks Seaside Town, Mountain Trail, and Forest Camp. Purchase and restore need a connection; after verification, you can continue exploring offline.

旅程、存档或完整版购买遇到问题？请发送邮件至 luoyi9932@gmail.com，附上 iPhone 型号、iOS 版本及问题简述。请勿发送银行卡资料。

恢复购买

在游戏设置中打开“完整版”，点击“恢复购买”。请使用原来购买的 Apple 账户并连接网络。恢复购买只恢复付费内容资格，不恢复旅行进度。

备份与恢复进度

在游戏设置中导出 JSON 存档并自行保存。在另一台设备导入文件并确认后，原有本机进度会被替换。请妥善保管备份文件。

离线游玩

城市公园可以免费探索。一次性购买完整版可解锁海边小镇、山间步道和森林营地。购买及恢复需要联网；验证后可继续离线探索。
