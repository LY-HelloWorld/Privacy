Privacy Policy

Little Chestnut's Journey is a game by huangxuemei. Effective November 14, 2026.

What the game stores

Your travel progress, collected stories, photos, keepsakes, and settings are saved on your device. The game does not require an account and does not send this game data to our servers. If you choose to export a save file, you control where that file is stored or shared. Importing a file replaces your local game progress only after you confirm.

Purchases

The optional Full Game purchase and Restore Purchases feature use Apple's StoreKit. Apple processes the transaction and provides the app with a verified purchase entitlement. We do not receive your payment card details. Purchase and restore require a network connection; the game can be played offline after purchase verification.

Notifications and analytics

If you enable trip notifications, they are scheduled on your device. The game does not use third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs.

Your choices

You can export or import your save in Settings. You can turn off notifications in iOS Settings. Deleting the app removes its local save unless you kept an exported backup; purchase entitlement can be restored through Apple using the same Apple Account.

Contact

For privacy questions, email luoyi9932@gmail.com.

隐私政策

小栗的远行由 huangxuemei 提供。本政策自 2026 年 11 月 14 日起生效。

本机存档

旅程进度、故事、照片、纪念品和设置保存在你的设备上。游戏无需注册账号，也不会把这些游戏数据发送到我们的服务器。你可以自行导出存档并决定保存或分享位置；导入存档前，游戏会要求你确认。

应用内购买

购买完整版与恢复购买通过 Apple StoreKit 处理。Apple 负责交易，并向游戏提供已验证的购买资格；我们不会收到你的银行卡资料。购买及恢复需要网络连接，验证后可离线游玩。

通知与分析

如果你启用旅行通知，通知会在本机安排。游戏不使用第三方广告、分析或追踪 SDK。

你的选择

你可以在设置中导出或导入存档，也可以在 iOS 设置中关闭通知。删除应用会移除本机存档，除非你另行保留了导出的备份；购买资格可使用同一 Apple 账户恢复。

联系
