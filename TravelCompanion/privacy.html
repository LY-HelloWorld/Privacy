A QUIET JOURNEY

Privacy, kept close to home.

Little Chestnut's Journey is an offline travel and collection game. The game is designed to keep your journey, collection, and cottage memories on your device.

Effective date: November 16, 2026
Bundle identifier: com.ly.TravelCompanion

What the game stores

The game stores your local game state on your device: travel progress, selected luggage, destinations, photos and keepsakes you have discovered, cottage placements, trail progress, friend relationships, saved memories, audio preferences, and any name you choose for the traveler.

No account and no game server

The game does not require an account and does not operate a server for your game progress. Travel results are generated and saved locally. We do not receive your journal, friend conversations, cottage layout, or collection through the game.

Backups are your choice

When you export a JSON backup, you choose where iOS saves the file. When you restore a backup, the game reads the file you select. A copy of the previous local save may be kept on the device before a restore. Files you export are controlled by the destination you choose, such as Files, iCloud Drive, or a person you share them with.

Do not share a backup file if it contains a name or game history you want to keep private. The game does not upload backups for you.

Notifications

If you choose a return reminder, iOS schedules a local notification on your device. The reminder contains the destination and a return message. No remote push server is used. You can change notification permission in iOS Settings.

Purchases

The game offers one non-consumable purchase that unlocks the full journey. Apple processes payment and purchase records through StoreKit. We receive the verified entitlement needed to unlock the paid regions; we do not receive your payment card number. The purchase is separate from the game save, so importing a save cannot grant paid access. You can use the in-game Restore Purchases action.

Audio, haptics, and device services

Music, ambient sound, effects, and haptics are generated and played locally. Audio settings are stored on the device. The game does not request camera, microphone, contacts, location, photos, or motion permissions.

Data collection and advertising

The game does not include advertising, third-party analytics, cross-app tracking, an account system, or a data broker. It does not intentionally collect your journal, photos, location, contacts, or other personal content.

Deletion

You can delete local progress through the game’s reset option, delete exported backups from their chosen locations, or delete the app and its local data through iOS. Apple purchase entitlement is managed by Apple and can be restored separately from game progress.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from children. It does not require an account or direct messaging.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the game or its platform services change. The effective date above will be updated with a material revision. For privacy questions, contact luoyi9932@gmail.com.
