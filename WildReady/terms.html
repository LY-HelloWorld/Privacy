Using maps and instruments

Check the map’s coverage, scale and data date before your trip. Routes and conditions can change. GPS fixes may be old or imprecise, and heading and altitude depend on the hardware and surroundings. A straight-line measurement does not establish a passable route. Carry navigation equipment suitable for your trip and follow current local instructions.

Guides and emergency signals

The guides provide general educational information. Consult the source and regional scope of each article and follow current instructions from local authorities. The SOS light and screen display produce a visual signal only. They do not call emergency services, send coordinates or notify a contact. If you need emergency assistance, contact the appropriate local service using an available means of communication.
