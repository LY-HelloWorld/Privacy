Using maps and instruments

Check the map’s coverage, scale and data date before your trip. The detailed map coverage is limited to the region described in the App and Support page. Routes, closures, weather, terrain and local rules can change. GPS fixes may be old, approximate, stale or unavailable, and heading, altitude, pressure and motion readings depend on the hardware, permissions and surroundings. A straight-line measurement does not establish a passable route. Carry suitable backup equipment and follow current instructions from local authorities and land managers.
