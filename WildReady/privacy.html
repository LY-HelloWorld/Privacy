Privacy Policy

WildReady processes your field notes on your device.

Version 1.0 · Updated November 25, 2026

Your local records

WildReady stores your map view, marked places, recorded track, packing lists, search and reading preferences, favorites, and language settings on your device. The developer does not receive these records. WildReady uses no accounts, advertising or analytics services.

Location and motion

When you enable GPS or start a recording, iOS asks for location access. WildReady uses available coordinates, altitude, accuracy and heading to display your position and record your route. A recording you start can continue while the screen is locked. Pause or finish it in Explore. You can revoke location access in iOS Settings under Privacy & Security → Location Services. The tools use available motion and pressure measurements to display level, tilt and barometric readings. You can manage motion access in iOS privacy settings.

Sharing and backups

When you export a checklist, you choose which file location or app receives it. That destination manages its copy. Your device backup can contain session records under your Apple backup settings. The app excludes its local copies of map resources from backup.

Retention and deletion

Your saved records remain on your device between launches. To remove the app and its local records, choose Delete App in iOS. Offloading preserves app data. Manage previously exported files and device backups separately in the locations where you saved them.

Purchases and support

Apple handles App Store purchases. If you email support, the developer receives your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. These are used to respond to your request and retained as needed for that correspondence. To request deletion of support correspondence, email luoyi9932@gmail.com. Please omit precise locations and private itineraries unless needed for your request.
