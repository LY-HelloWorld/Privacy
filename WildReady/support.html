WildReady Support

Help with maps, recordings, offline reading and packing lists.

Version 1.0 · Updated November 25, 2026

What works without a connection?

The installed app includes the world overview, regional detail around Grindelwald, field guides and packing lists. The world overview contains data through zoom level 5. Grindelwald has detail through level 14. Zooming farther enlarges the available data.

How do I start or stop GPS recording?

In Explore, open the recording controls and start a track. Allow location access when prompted. Pause to take a break in the track, or finish to end recording. Check the time and accuracy of the last fix. You can review permissions in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services.

Why is an instrument unavailable?

Some readings need specific hardware or a usable sensor measurement. For heading, enable location services and move away from magnetic interference. The app shows an unavailable reading when iOS cannot supply one.

How do I export my checklist?

Open Gear and select the list. Choose Export to share text or Save PDF to choose a destination in Files. The exported copy reflects your list at the time of export.

Can I read in another language?

Open Guide, expand Library options and select a reading language. The separate interface language setting is in Resources and settings.
