How to remove a watermark from a photo on iPhone

For an image you are allowed to edit, open the photo, select the watermark removal mode, brush tightly over the visible mark, preview the repair, refine rough areas, and export. If a small mark sits at the edge, cropping may produce a cleaner result.

What affects cleanup quality?

The tool rebuilds pixels behind the selected area. The less predictable that hidden detail is, the more carefully the result needs inspection.

Better fit

Small marks with nearby background

  • Corner marks over sky, wall, or tabletop
  • Compact overlays away from faces
  • Marks that cannot be cropped without harming composition
Harder case

Large or translucent overlays

  • Marks crossing faces, hair, or hands
  • Patterns, fine text, fences, and straight edges
  • Overlays covering information that is not visible elsewhere

Questions about watermark removal

How do I remove a watermark from a photo on iPhone?

Open an image you own or have permission to edit, choose the watermark removal mode, brush tightly over the mark, preview the result, refine rough areas, and export.

Should I crop or repair a corner watermark?

Crop when the mark is at the edge and losing that area will not hurt the composition. Use removal when cropping would cut out important content.

Can every watermark be removed cleanly?

No. Large or translucent marks crossing faces, hair, textural patterns, or important edges may leave visible artifacts and need smaller passes.

Can I remove a watermark from any image I find online?

No. Only edit images you own or have permission to modify. Do not remove watermarks from unpaid proofs, stock previews, school photos, or protected content.
