How to remove a date stamp from a photo on iPhone

If the date is visibly printed into the photo, open an authorized image, choose Date Stamp mode, select the date, preview the repaired corner, refine it if needed, and export. This removes visible pixels; it does not change the photo's EXIF capture date.

Visible date stamp versus EXIF date

These are two different kinds of information and require different tools.

Visible pixels

A date you can see

Older cameras and camera apps may print numerals directly into the image. Date Stamp mode repairs those selected pixels.

Photo metadata

A capture date stored as EXIF

EXIF can contain capture time even when no date appears in the picture. Removing visible pixels does not edit or erase that metadata.

Questions about removing date stamps

How do I remove a date stamp from a photo on iPhone?

Open an authorized photo, select Date Stamp mode, mark the visible date, preview the reconstructed corner, refine if needed, and export.

Is a visible date stamp the same as EXIF date metadata?

No. A visible stamp is burned into the image pixels. EXIF is stored as metadata and can remain even when the printed date is removed.

Which date stamps are easiest to remove?

Small corner dates over a consistent background are usually easier. Dates crossing people, patterned clothing, or detailed scenery need closer inspection.

Is smart date removal a free feature?

Smart date removal is a Pro feature. The app also provides standard export and one HD export free; check the current App Store offer for exact purchase options.
