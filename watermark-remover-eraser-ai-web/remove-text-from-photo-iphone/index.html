When text removal needs extra care

Cleanup quality depends on what sits behind the letters. No removal tool can promise a perfect reconstruction in every photo.

Usually easier

Text over a simple background

  • Small captions on sky or a plain wall
  • Labels surrounded by consistent color
  • Short text away from the subject
Needs inspection

Text crossing important detail

  • Faces, hair, hands, or clothing seams
  • Brick, foliage, grids, or repeating patterns
  • Large letters covering multiple edges

Questions about removing text from photos

Can I remove text that is part of a photo on iPhone?

Yes. When the text is embedded in the image pixels, select Text mode, brush over the letters, preview the repaired area, and refine the selection before exporting.

Does this change editable text or photo metadata?

No. This workflow repairs visible pixels. It does not edit a text layer, OCR document, caption field, or EXIF metadata.

Why can removed text leave a blurry or repeated patch?

Text crossing a face, hair, sharp edge, or repeating pattern gives the repair model less clean background to reconstruct. Use a tight selection and make smaller passes.

Are photos processed on the iPhone?

Core image removal is processed on-device. Ancillary app services such as purchases and analytics may still use network services as described in the privacy policy.
